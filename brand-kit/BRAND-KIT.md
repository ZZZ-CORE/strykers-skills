## Logo explanation (page 4)

The primary logo is a shield crest: a castle crown on top, a castle wall with a sunrise and purple mountains, "EMPIRE" and "STRYKERS" lettering, and a soccer ball with a star at the bottom. What each part means:

| Part | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| "EMPIRE" lettering | The community we aim to represent |
| Castle wall with sunrise and purple mountains | The bright and strong future of our club and region |
| Jet / arrow mark (also the secondary logo) | A symbol of our fast and growing market, and a tribute to our beloved Joseph "The Jet" Cairel |
| "STRYKERS" lettering | The name of our mascot and our fast attacking style of play |
| Castle crown | A symbol of our strength to fight for what we believe in |
| Ball with star | A symbol of our innovation to be revolutionary in and for our sport |

## Primary logo usage (page 6)

- Versions: **full color**, or one color in **White, Silver Sand, Aluminium, Mid Grey, Black, Plum, or Rouge**.
- A one-color version may be used **only if the printing or reproduction process limits the number of colors, and only if approved by the Strykers**.
- Always keep **sufficient contrast** between the background and the logo.
- The examples show the light/grey versions on a Plum background and the Plum/Rouge versions on Black.

## Secondary logo usage (page 7)

The secondary logo is the jet / arrow mark.

- On photographic backgrounds, graphics, or merchandise, use **full color** or one color in **White, Silver Sand, Aluminium, Mid Grey, Black, Plum, or Rouge**.
- Other colorways may be used in a rough draft only; **final use must be approved by the Strykers**.
- **Stroke thickness: minimum 0.5, maximum 3.0.**

## Co-branded logo lockups (page 11)

Logos are separated by thin divider lines.

- **2 logos total:**
  - Horizontal: Strykers logo on the left, divider, partner logo on the right.
  - Vertical: Strykers logo on top, divider, partner logo below.
- **3 or more logos total:**
  - Odd number of logos: the **Strykers logo goes in the center**.
  - Even number of logos: the **Strykers logo goes on the left**.

Examples on the page use the MASL (Major Arena Soccer League), TST, and Verizon logos.

## Brand messaging (pages 12–13)

**Tagline:** Empire Strykers: Inspiring the Empire Through Pro Arena Soccer

Consider these pillars for all promotional content: social media captions, ads, newsletters, pitch decks, emails, and so on.

**Mission statement:** The Empire Strykers' mission is to unite and inspire the Inland Empire community through the power of sports, with a focus on professional indoor soccer. We strive to be more than just a team; we aim to be a premier sports entertainment experience while actively contributing to the region's growth, health, and development.

**Motto: Inspire The Empire.** It isn't just a motto; it's a movement. It reflects our commitment to: be the premier team in the MASL; develop professional players and personnel; embrace and inspire our community; provide a family-friendly experience to all; deliver championship-caliber performance; and Inspire The Next Generation.

**Frequently used phrases:**
Defend The Empire · Rise With Empire · We Are Empire · Beware The Empire · Family Friendly · Inspire Your Empire · Inspire The Empire · Greatest Sport On Turf · The American Fans Sport · Pro Arena Soccer · Futbol Rapido Profesional · Inland Empire's Only True Professional Team · Future Of American Soccer

**Frequently used words:**
Enjoy · Community · Professional · Represent · Youth · Premier · Electric · Non Stop · Inspire · Believe · Support · Exciting · Defend · Stryke · Score

> "Community impact and the unique excitement of pro arena soccer"

**Brand identity and messaging pillars:**

- **Community First, Always:** More than a team: a community force. The brand emphasizes accessibility, inclusion, and uplifting the Inland Empire, bringing pro sports back to the local community and creating new hometown heroes for youth to look up to.
- **Fast, Fierce, and Fun:** Pro arena soccer's high energy, fast pace, flashy goals, and physical intensity: the Strykers are the definition of a thrilling fan experience that stands toe-to-toe with any league or team.
- **Underdog Spirit with Big League Swagger:** Blue-collar grit with ambitious vision, often framing the Strykers as a team "fighting to make the Empire & MASL proud again" and bring a championship back home. A clear redemption arc, a hunger to prove ourselves, and a constant push to put the MASL and the Strykers on the global soccer map.
- **Inspired by Legacy, Building for the Future:** Thinking back to the early MISL glory days, the Strykers want to bring that back with a new culture of modern, diverse, and media-savvy soccer and content.
- **Open Doors, Open Hearts:** A transparent, inviting culture where everyone has a role in building the Empire.
- **Content That Connects:** Trendy, relatable, edgy content and language to connect with younger and all audiences while elevating player personalities.
- **Inspire The Empire:** A call to action for players, staff, and fans to uplift the entire Inland Empire community through passion, effort, and pride, on and off the field. Representing something bigger than yourself: a region, a movement, a shared dream.
- **Inspire Your Empire:** A motivational message for individuals (a player, a kid in the stands, a fan) to take ownership of their goals, dreams, and influence. It reframes "Empire" as your personal kingdom, your world, your legacy.

**Be bold. Be original. Bring heritage. Bring futuristic flair.**

## Pro arena soccer terms (page 16)

Branded terms, listed in their correct spelling:

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | When a team / player puts the ball in the opposing team's goal |
| Hattrick | 3 goals in 1 game for 1 player |
| Wallascora | A goal where the assist bounced off the wall/boards before touching the goal scorer |
| Power Play | The time when a team has a numerical player advantage over the other team |
| Power Kill | The time when a team has a numerical player disadvantage against the other team |
| 6th Attacker | When a team pulls its goalkeeper for another field player |
| Golaso | A beautiful goal |
| Golazo | A beautiful goal when the match is being aired on CBS Sports Golazo |
| Blue Card | Foul where the player has to sit out for 2 minutes |
| Yellow Card | Dissent from a player that results in sitting out for 5 minutes |
| Red Card | Ejected from the match |
| Fan Fest | A big, multiple-fan-activation event outside of a game |
| Overtime | An additional 5th 15-minute quarter; first team to score wins |
