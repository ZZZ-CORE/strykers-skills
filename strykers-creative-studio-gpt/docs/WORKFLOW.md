# Production Workflow

## Intake

- Campaign goal and platform
- Source MP4(s)
- Event date, venue, city, opponent, and ticket CTA
- Approved logos, sponsors, fonts, and music
- Deadline and required deliverables

## Creative pass

- Identify the strongest authentic moments.
- Build a 20–25 second vertical treatment.
- Keep copy legible and leave an approved logo-safe area.
- Mark each factual item as verified, user-supplied, or `NEEDS APPROVAL`.

## Technical pass

- Confirm 1080×1920 output and playable H.264 video.
- Confirm 20–25 second duration unless the brief overrides it.
- Confirm multiple distinct source moments and at least 80% source-footage screen time.
- Review representative frames for crop quality, identity preservation, typography, and logo-safe space.

## Handoff

Provide the final absolute file path, creative summary, export metadata, source/music disclosures, and remaining verification flags.
